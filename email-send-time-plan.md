# Send-Time Plan — Book 5 Consults in 5 Days, September 7–11, 2026

Recommended send times for the new "JK New Emails & Texts" sequence, built from three rounds of our own performance data and current external benchmarks.

All times are **Pacific**. Add 3 hours for Eastern. Live calls are 10:00am PT / 1:00pm ET.

---

## 1. Two calendar conflicts that change the plan

These are the first things to settle, because they move more than any send time will.

**Day 1 is Labor Day (Monday, September 7, 2026).** Day-1 show-up is our single biggest conversion lever — 15.74% in July, 18.28% in August, against a 20–25% benchmark — and we are opening on a federal holiday when a large share of the list is travelling or with family. Options, in order of preference:

1. Keep the date and over-invest in the Sunday-evening and Monday-morning touches (built into the schedule below), and lean the copy into it: one hour on a holiday morning, then the rest of the day is yours.
2. Accept a lower Day 1 and plan for Day 2 as the real recovery point. This is defensible — the August round won on the *mid-week* curve (Day 4 = 10 sign-ups, Day 5 = 9, where July was 0 and 0), not on Day 1.

**Rosh Hashanah runs from sundown Friday September 11 to nightfall Sunday September 13.** That covers Day 5 evening, all of Day 6, and all of Day 7 — including the **Sunday 9pm PT deadline** for replays coming down and Quest completion. Observant participants cannot watch a replay or finish a Quest during the exact window we have made the deadline, and Quest completion gates Grand Prize eligibility.

**Recommendation:** extend the replay + Quest deadline to **Monday, September 14, 9:00am PT**, and say so in the Day 6 and Day 7 emails. It costs nothing, it protects Grand Prize eligibility for a segment we would otherwise silently exclude, and it gives Monday's Grand Finale email a second reason to be opened.

## 2. Four rules that drive every time below

**Rule 1 — Move the daily morning email from 7:00am to 6:15am.**

Opens peak between 8 and 11am in the recipient's local time. A 7:00am PT send lands at 10:00am ET, which is both the tail of that window for our largest time zones and the single most crowded slot in email marketing — 10am Tuesday is the most-cited "best time" in every benchmark study, which is exactly why it is the hardest slot to stand out in. 6:15am PT lands at 9:15am ET / 8:15am CT / 7:15am MT, inside or just ahead of the peak in all four zones, and it is first in the inbox on the West Coast.

The `:15` is deliberate. Our own highest-open historical sends used 6:15am and 8:15am slots, and an off-the-hour send avoids the top-of-hour queue crush at the ESP.

**Rule 2 — Minimum 90 minutes between any two sends to the same person. Hard cap of 4 emails per day, except Day 1 and Day 9.**

July delivery was 98.35% against a 99% target, unsubscribes ran 0.58% against a 0.30% target, and the new sending domain is still warming (flagged open into C3). Bunched sends are the fastest way to convert a warming domain into a spam-folder problem. Volume is not our issue — inbox placement is. Click-to-open held flat at 6.04% vs 5.96% in March, which tells us the copy works *once it lands*.

**Rule 3 — SMS only between 8:00am and 5:45pm PT.**

TCPA bans marketing texts outside 8am–9pm in the *recipient's* local time, and Texas requires 9am–9pm. 5:45pm PT is 8:45pm ET — the last minute that is compliant coast to coast. Penalties run $500 per message, $1,500 for willful violations. Every text in the current draft is compliant; the constraint only bites if we try to add a final-hours text on Day 9 (see Day 9 below).

**Rule 4 — Anchor reminders to the event, everything else to the inbox.**

The 1-hour (9:00am) and 15-minute (9:45am) reminders stay fixed because the call is at 10:00am. Content, replay and offer emails move to inbox-optimal slots. The 15-minute reminder is the one to protect: it produces the single biggest spike in logins, and a three-reminder sequence (day before / 1 hour / 15 min) lifts live attendance roughly 27% over sending fewer.

## 3. The schedule

### Pre-challenge — rolling drip, triggered off opt-in

| Send | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Email 1 — You're IN | Immediately on opt-in |
| Text 1 — You're officially in | Immediately, **or queued to 8:00am recipient local time** if the opt-in lands outside 8am–8pm local |
| Email 2 — I'd love to know what you do | Opt-in + 1 day, 6:15am |
| Email 3 — Standing outside Safeway | Opt-in + 2 days, 6:15am |
| Email 4 — Why aren't they noticing you? | Opt-in + 3 days, 6:15am |
| Email 5 — OMG! I booked 5 consults | Opt-in + 4 days, 6:15am |
| Email 6 — 3X Your Consults | Opt-in + 5 days, 6:15am |
| **Drip hard stop** | **Sat Sept 5, 9:00pm** |

Email 1 is our best-performing send of the entire round and should stay immediate — it is transactional in the reader's mind. Note its open rate has decayed steadily: 55–57% through 2025, then 42.24% in January, 38.51% in April, 37.80% in May, 35.98% in July. That is a deliverability trend, not a copy trend, and no send time will fix it.

### Pre-challenge — fixed date, everyone regardless of drip position

| Send | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Text 2 — I want to know what you do | Sat Sept 5, 9:00am |
| Email 7 — The Night Nobody Showed Up | Sun Sept 6, 8:00am |
| Email — Tomorrow, this gets real | Sun Sept 6, 4:30pm |
| Text 3 — Day before | Sun Sept 6, 5:15pm |

Email 7 is the emotional peak of the pre-challenge run and must be pinned to Sunday rather than left to drift in the drip. Sunday late afternoon is when people plan their week, and clicks peak in the evening — that is the right slot for "Tomorrow, this gets real," not the morning.

**Three fixes here.**

*The Day 1 morning email is missing.* Days 2 through 5 each open with a 7:00am email. Day 1 — the day with the most show-up leverage in the whole round — currently has nothing until 9:00am PT, which is noon Eastern. Someone on the East Coast wakes up on launch day and hears nothing from us for six hours. Add a 6:15am send.

*The Day 1 text is running ahead of the email.* It currently goes at 11:00am and says "the replay is up now," but the replay email does not go until 11:45am. Move it to 12:15pm so the text reinforces the email instead of pre-empting it.

*Answering Sharla's question directly — yes, 11:15am is too soon,* but for an operational reason rather than an engagement one. 11:45am is right. The extra 30 minutes is the buffer that lets us confirm the replay is actually live in the Hub before we email 2,500 people a link to it. Broken gift and replay links have been a recurring P1 across the last three rounds; this is the cheapest place to buy insurance against another one.

*Day 1 Email 4 ("And… it's gone")* should only fire at 5:00pm if the 15 bonus spots have genuinely filled. If they have not, hold it to Day 2 at 6:15am and adjust the copy. Sending a scarcity email that is not true is more expensive than sending it late.

Day 1 now carries 6 emails plus 2 texts. That is a deliberate peak — and it is one message lighter than July, because the deposit email has moved.

| Day 9 Email 4 — 2 hours left | 7:00pm | was untimed |
| *Optional* — 15 minutes left | 8:45pm | |

Four emails on the final day is the standard for a reason: the majority of launch revenue arrives in the last two days, and disproportionately in the final hours. Our own archive agrees — the Day 9 4:00pm send in September 2023 produced the best click rate of that day, and the "your $3,900 scholarship is expiring" deadline email produced 2.33% clicks against a 0.4–0.7% baseline for the same sequence.

Two hard requirements for this day:

- **No text after 4:00pm.** 4:00pm PT is 7:00pm ET. A text at 7:15pm PT would be 10:15pm ET and outside the legal window for every Eastern recipient.
- **Deduplicate Open Cart and Summit Click.** In July both sequences fired to the same people on the final day. One sequence has to yield to the other. A participant should not get the 11:00am FOMO email and a parallel Summit Click send.

Tuesday is the strongest day of the week in every benchmark for both opens and clicks. Closing on a Tuesday is a tailwind — worth protecting in future rounds.

## 4. What to measure, and what to ignore

**Ignore open rate.** Apple Mail Privacy Protection accounted for 64.66% of our opens as of May. The 25.4% July open rate is not a real number and cannot be compared across rounds where the Apple share moves.

**Judge these changes on three things:**

1. **Click-to-open rate** on the daily emails — the only email metric that survives Apple. July was 6.04%, March 5.96%.
2. **Day 1 show-up rate** — 15.74% July, 18.28% August, benchmark 20–25%.
3. **Summit sign-ups by day** — the shape matters more than the total. August won because Days 4, 5, 7 and 9 stayed alive (10, 9, 7, 8) where July went to zero mid-week.

**Before any of this can be measured, fix tracking.** July had 36.67% of email rows missing click data, against 5.71% in March, plus untracked UTMs. Above roughly 20% missing, click rate and click-to-open are understated and no send-time comparison means anything. This is the prerequisite, not a parallel task.

**Hold the times fixed for two rounds.** Changing send times and copy and channel mix in the same round produces a result nobody can attribute. If we want one clean experiment, split the Day 2–5 morning email 50/50 between 6:15am and 7:00am and read it on click-to-open, with everything else held constant.

## 5. Summary of changes

**Timing changes**

- Daily morning email: 7:00am → 6:15am
- Day 1 text: 11:00am → 12:15pm (now follows the replay email instead of preceding it)
- Deposit explainer: Day 1 3:30pm → Day 2 1:45pm
- Day 4 replay: 11:15am → 11:45am (consistent with every other day)
- Prize emails: standardised at 1:45pm (Day 2 at 3:45pm behind the deposit email)
- Day 6 first email: 9:00am → 8:00am
- Day 6 / 7 second emails: 2:00pm → 1:45pm

**Gaps to fill**

- Day 1 morning email at 6:15am
- Day 4 and Day 5 texts at 9:30am
- Day 7 text at 5:30pm
- Day 8 "starts in 15 minutes" email at 9:45am
- Times assigned to all Day 8 and Day 9 emails
- Numbering: the draft jumps from Day 1 Email 2 to Day 1 Email 4 — confirm nothing is missing

**Rules to apply across the board**

- 90 minutes minimum between sends; 4 emails/day cap outside Days 1 and 9
- SMS only 8:00am–5:45pm PT
- Open Cart / Summit Click deduplication on Day 9

**Decisions needed**

- Labor Day on Day 1 — hold the date and over-invest in Sunday/Monday touches, or move?
- Extend the replay and Quest deadline to Monday 9:00am PT for Rosh Hashanah?
- Day 2 at 5 emails, or cut the 9:45am reminder and let the 9:30am text carry it?
