## Four rules behind the times

1. **Morning email at 6:15am, not 7:00am.** 7am PT is 10am ET — the tail of the 8–11am open window and the most crowded slot in email. 6:15am PT = 9:15 ET / 8:15 CT, and first in the inbox out west.
2. **90 minutes minimum between sends. Max 4 emails a day** outside Days 1 and 9. July delivery was 98.35% against a 99% target and the sending domain is still warming.
3. **SMS only 8:00am–5:45pm PT.** TCPA is 8am–9pm in the recipient's local time; 5:45pm PT is 8:45pm ET, the last minute that works coast to coast.
4. **Reminders stay pinned to the 10:00am call** (9:00am and 9:45am). Everything else moves to inbox-best slots.
